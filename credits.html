FOUNDATIONAL LITERATURE

Core Academic Textbooks

Craig, John J. (2018) Introduction to Robotics: Mechanics and Control (4th Edition). Pearson Education. Used for Denavit-Hartenberg (D-H) kinematics parameters and coordinate transformation matrices.
Spong, Mark W., Hutchinson, Seth, & Vidyasagar, M. (2020) Robot Modeling and Control (2nd Edition). John Wiley & Sons. Reference for Euler-Lagrange equations, Jacobian singular analysis, and inverse kinematics algorithms.
Siciliano, Bruno, & Khatib, Oussama (Eds.) (2016) Springer Handbook of Robotics (2nd Edition). Springer-Verlag Berlin Heidelberg. Reference for surgical teleoperation haptics and Zero Moment Point (ZMP) bipedal gait balance.
Thrun, Sebastian, Burgard, Wolfram, & Fox, Dieter (2005) Probabilistic Robotics. The MIT Press. Foundational literature for LiDAR SLAM, Extended Kalman Filters (EKF), and grid path planning heuristics.
INDUSTRIAL PROTOCOLS & SAFETY

International Engineering Standards

  • ISO 13849-1 / IEC 62061 (Functional Safety): Principles for Category 4 / Performance Level e (PLe) safety-related parts of control systems, light curtains, and emergency stop dual-channel interlocks.
  • ISO 10218-1 / ISO 10218-2: Safety requirements for industrial robots, robotic devices, and integrated work cells.
  • ISO 11898-1:2015 (Road Vehicles — CAN): Controller Area Network (CAN 2.0B) data link layer and physical signaling protocol specifications.
  • ROS 2 Architecture & Repositories: Open Robotics ROS 2 Humble Hawksbill core communications specifications (rclpy, geometry_msgs, sensor_msgs).
