CHAPTER 02 // TAXONOMY

Classification of Robots

Robots are categorized by mechanical topology, degrees of freedom (DOF), and locomotion method to suit diverse operating environments.

MANIPULATION

Industrial Manipulators

Fixed-base articulated, SCARA, and Delta robots engineered for high-speed repeatability (±0.01 mm) in automated manufacturing.

  • Kinematics: Denavit-Hartenberg (D-H) parameter models.
  • DOF: 4 to 7 serial or parallel rotary joints.
  • Key Tasks: Spot welding, painting, pick-and-place, CNC feeding.
FANUC, KUKA, ABB 0.01mm TOLERANCE
NAVIGATION

Autonomous Mobile (AMRs)

Wheeled and tracked platforms using 2D/3D LiDAR Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) for warehouse and factory transport.

  • Steering: Differential drive or Mecanum omnidirectional.
  • Planning: A* global path + Dynamic Window local avoidance.
  • Safety: PL-d rated optical safety laser zones.
AMAZON KIVA, MIR UP TO 1500 KG
BIOMIMICRY

Humanoid Robotics

Anthropomorphic bipedal morphology configured to navigate human spaces, climb stairs, open doors, and operate human tools.

  • Locomotion: Zero Moment Point (ZMP) & Model Predictive Control.
  • Hands: Dexterous tendon-driven 5-finger tactile arrays.
  • AI Core: Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models.
FIGURE 01, ATLAS 28-54+ DOF
HEALTHCARE

Surgical Tele-Robots

Master-slave teleoperation systems that eliminate human hand tremors and scale surgeon motion down 5:1 for laparoscopic procedures.

  • Haptics: Bilateral impedance force feedback.
  • Wrist: 7-DOF EndoWrist mimicking human hand rotation.
  • Vision: Stereoscopic 3D 4K magnified real-time feed.
DA VINCI, MAKO SUB-MM ACCURACY
AEROSPACE

Aerial Drones & UAVs

Multirotor autonomous aerial systems with fast-loop cascaded PID attitude stabilization and optical flow navigation.

  • Autopilot: STM32 running PX4 or ArduPilot firmware.
  • State Estimator: Extended Kalman Filter (EKF2).
  • Actuation: High-speed brushless motors with digital ESCs.
SKYDIO, INGENUITY 8 KHZ GYRO LOOP
COLLECTIVE

Swarm Robotics

Decentralized ensembles of simple robots coordinating through local peer-to-peer sensing to accomplish emergent tasks.

  • Architecture: Zero single-point-of-failure mesh.
  • Algorithms: Flocking (Boids), consensus, distributed mapping.
  • Use Cases: Search and rescue, environmental surveys.
KILOBOTS, INTEL 1000+ AGENTS
