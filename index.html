CORE PRINCIPLE

The Sense — Plan — Act Paradigm

All intelligent robots operate on this continuous cybernetic feedback loop.

1

SENSE

Cameras, LiDAR, IMUs, and encoders perceive distances, orientation, and obstacles.

2

PLAN

The controller computes collision-free trajectories and solves Inverse Kinematics.

3

ACT

PWM signals modulate current to servomotors and drivers to move the linkages.

TIMELINE

Milestones in Robotics

1920

Origin of "Robot"

Karel Čapek premiers R.U.R. in Prague, deriving "robot" from the Slavic robota (forced servitude).

1954

Unimate Industrial Arm

George Devol and Joseph Engelberger patent Unimate, deployed on a GM diecasting line in 1961.

1969

The Stanford Arm

Victor Scheinman invents the first computer-controlled 6-axis all-electric arm, establishing modern kinematics.

1997

Sojourner on Mars

NASA deploys the first autonomous planetary rover on Mars with autonomous hazard avoidance.

2000

ASIMO & da Vinci Surgery

Honda reveals dynamic bipedal balance, while Intuitive Surgical earns FDA approval for robotic surgery.

2026+

Physical AI & Humanoids

Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models give humanoids the ability to understand and manipulate everyday objects.
