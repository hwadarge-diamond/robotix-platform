FIRST PRINCIPLES

The Cybernetic Feedback Loop

Every autonomous robot operates through this continuous three-phase loop:

1

SENSE

LiDAR, stereo vision, and optical encoders convert physical phenomena into digital voltages.

2

PLAN

Embedded microcontrollers evaluate trajectory kinematics, PID error, and SLAM maps.

3

ACT

PWM signals modulate power MOSFET H-bridges to energize joint servomotors.

PHILOSOPHY & BRANDING

The Nexus Emblem — The Meaning of the Robot Logo

The ROBOTIX Nexus emblem was engineered to symbolize the convergence of mechanical design, digital electronics, and software autonomy:

01. The Tri-Ring Nexus

The interlocking rings represent the unbroken Sense — Plan — Act feedback loop bridging Mechanical, Electrical, and Computer Engineering.

02. The Optical Hex-Core

The central pupil represents machine vision — LiDAR optics, camera arrays, and optical encoders translating photons into spatial understanding.

03. The Circuit Traces

The delicate branching traces symbolize digital electronic logic, microsecond PWM switching, and the synthetic nervous system.
