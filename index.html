01 // FOUNDATIONS

What is Robotics?

Robotics is the multidisciplinary branch of engineering and computer science dedicated to the conception, design, manufacture, and operational intelligence of mechanical agents capable of interacting with the physical world.

Mechanical Body

Chassis, linkages, gear trains, harmonic drives, and end-effectors engineered with rigid materials, kinematics synthesis, and structural stress analysis.

PHYSICAL EMBODIMENT

Electrical Subsystems

Power distribution, motor drivers (H-Bridges), sensor transducers (LiDAR, IMUs), power converters, and high-speed communication buses (CAN, SPI).

ENERGY & SIGNALS

Computational Intelligence

The classic "Sense-Plan-Act" cycle implemented across RTOS microcontrollers and GPU edge computers running SLAM, path planning, and neural policies.

AUTONOMY & CONTROL
CYBERNETIC FEEDBACK ARCHITECTURE

The Sense — Plan — Act Paradigm

1
SENSE
Perceive external environment via LiDAR, IMU, cameras, and encoders.
2

Compute trajectory, resolve collision constraints, solve Inverse Kinematics.
3
ACT
Send PWM voltage pulses to motors and servo drives to effect motion.
CHRONICLES OF AUTOMATION

Pioneering Milestones

1920

Etymology of the "Robot"

Czech playwright Karel Čapek premieres R.U.R. (Rossum's Universal Robots), introducing the word "robot" from the Slavic robota, meaning forced labor or servitude.

1954

Unimate: The First Industrial Arm

George Devol and Joseph Engelberger patent and build the Unimate, installed on a General Motors diecasting line in 1961 to lift scorching hot metal parts safely.

1969

The Stanford Arm (Scheinman)

Victor Scheinman invents the Stanford Arm: the first computer-controlled, 6-axis, all-electric robot arm, establishing the kinematics basis for all modern industrial manipulators.

1997

Sojourner on Mars

NASA deploys Sojourner on Mars via Mars Pathfinder. With a rocker-bogie suspension and semi-autonomous laser hazard avoidance, planetary rover exploration begins.

2000

Honda ASIMO & da Vinci Surgery

Honda reveals ASIMO demonstrating dynamic bipedal walking balance, while Intuitive Surgical earns FDA approval for the da Vinci Surgical System, pioneering robotic teleoperation.

2024+

Embodied AI & Humanoids

Integration of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models into agile humanoids (Boston Dynamics Atlas, Figure 01, Tesla Optimus) transforming unstructured real-world dexterity.
