CHAPTER 04 // CIRCUITS, SEMICONDUCTORS & SIGNALS

Digital Electronics in Robotics

Digital electronics forms the nervous system of modern robots. Explore how logic gates, MOSFET H-bridges, ADCs, quadrature decoders, and serial buses translate code into physical power.

SEMICONDUCTOR ACTUATION

1. H-Bridge Transistor Motor Driver

An H-Bridge uses 4 power MOSFET switches (Q1 to Q4) to reverse motor polarity and voltage without mechanical relays.

TRANSISTOR STATES:
Q1 (High-Side L): ON (CLOSED)
Q2 (Low-Side L): OFF (OPEN)
Q3 (High-Side R): OFF (OPEN)
Q4 (Low-Side R): ON (CLOSED)
Forward Mode: High-side switch Q1 and low-side switch Q4 are closed. Current flows from VCC (12V) through the motor armature from Left to Right, spinning the motor clockwise.
Shoot-Through Prevention: Q1 & Q2 (or Q3 & Q4) must NEVER be closed simultaneously to avoid dead short circuits.
SENSOR SAMPLING

2. ADC Sampling & Quantization

Microcontrollers cannot read continuous analog voltages directly. Successive Approximation (SAR) ADCs quantize analog signals into discrete binary numbers.

ANALOG INPUT SENSOR VOLTAGE (VIN): 1.65 V
0.00 V (GND) VREF = 3.30 V
MICROCONTROLLER REGISTER DATA:
0b10000000
DECIMAL: 128
LSB STEP: 12.89 mV
Quantization Formula: Digital Count = $\text{round}\left(\frac{V_{\text{IN}}}{V_{\text{REF}}} \cdot (2^N - 1)\right)$. Higher bit resolutions ($N=12$) decrease step size, providing sub-millivolt accuracy for analog distance sensors.
MOTION FEEDBACK

3. Quadrature Optical Encoder & 2-Bit State Machine

Two optical phototransistor channels (A and B) generate square waves offset by 90° electrical degrees (quadrature). This phase shift allows the microcontroller timer to determine rotational direction and count 4x pulses per physical cycle.

2-BIT GRAY CODE STATE TRANSITIONS:
S0 00
→
S1 01
→
S2 11
→
S3 10
• Clockwise (CW): State advances 00 → 01 → 11 → 10 (Channel A rises before Channel B).
• Counter-Clockwise (CCW): State reverses 10 → 11 → 01 → 00.
• 4X Multiplication: Every edge (both rising and falling of A and B) triggers a hardware counter interrupt, quadrupling resolution without changing the physical encoder disc.
COMMUNICATIONS

4. Serial Bus Waveform Analyzer

Inspect live synchronized digital oscilloscope timing signals for CAN Bus, SPI, I2C, and UART.

CAN Bus (Controller Area Network)

Differential two-wire bus (CAN_High and CAN_Low). When dominant (0), CAN_H rises to 3.5V and CAN_L drops to 1.5V (2.0V differential). When recessive (1), both stay at 2.5V (0V differential). Eliminates electromagnetic interference from motor windings.
