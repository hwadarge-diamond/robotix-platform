Robotics Microcontrollers & Buses

Comparison of common processors used in hobby and industrial robotic platforms.

STM32 / ARM

High-frequency deterministic RTOS execution. Perfect for flight controllers and motor FOC control.

ESP32

Dual-core with built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth. Ideal for IoT telemetry and wireless robot control.

Raspberry Pi 5

Full Linux Single Board Computer. Runs ROS 2, OpenCV computer vision, and LiDAR point clouds.

NVIDIA Jetson

CUDA-accelerated edge AI platform. Runs real-time deep neural networks and Isaac Sim policies.
