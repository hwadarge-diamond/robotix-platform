CHAPTER 01 // ANATOMY

Components of a Robot

Every autonomous robot is synthesized from five foundational subsystems. Click the interactive buttons below to inspect each subsystem on the 3D model.

SUBSYSTEM DOSSIER

Sensors: The Senses of the Machine

Sensory transducers measure environment metrics and internal joint positions, transforming optical, acoustic, and magnetic stimuli into calibrated digital readings.

TYPICAL HARDWARE LiDAR, Cameras, IMU, Ultrasonic
BUS PROTOCOLS I2C, SPI, CAN-FD, GigE Vision
UPDATE RATE 100 Hz – 1000 Hz
CRITICAL METRIC Sub-millimeter precision
Key Insight: Sensor fusion algorithms (like Extended Kalman Filters) merge fast, noisy IMU readings with slower absolute LiDAR frames to prevent robot positional drift.
01 // PERCEPTION

Sensory Transducers

Internal sensors (rotary encoders, current monitors) detect the robot's own joint angles and motor effort. External sensors (LiDAR, depth cameras, sonar) build spatial maps and detect dynamic obstacles.

02 // ACTUATION

Motors & Linkages

Brushless DC (BLDC) motors paired with zero-backlash harmonic drive reducers deliver the extreme torque density needed for high-speed repeatability in articulated arm joints.

03 // COMPUTATION

Embedded Controllers

A dual-tier compute architecture is standard: low-level ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers handle 1 kHz motor PID loops with microsecond determinism, while high-level SBCs run SLAM navigation.

04 // ENERGY

Power Regulation & BMS

High-discharge lithium chemistry packs (LiFePO4/LiPo) are paired with switching buck/boost converters to isolate microcontrollers from inductive voltage spikes generated by motors.
