Robotics Physics, Kinematics & Control

Real-time interactive engineering laboratories: closed-loop PID step response, 3-joint D-H kinematics with reachable workspace envelope, grid path planning with A*, ultrasonic rangefinding, and quadrature encoders.

CONTROL THEORY

1. Closed-Loop PID Controller Simulator

Tune Proportional ($K_p$), Integral ($K_i$), and Derivative ($K_d$) feedback gains to stabilize the joint actuator: $u(t) = K_p e(t) + K_i \int e(t)\,dt + K_d \frac{de(t)}{dt}$.

RISE TIME ($t_r$) 0.34 s
PEAK OVERSHOOT ($M_p$) 4.2 %
SETTLING TIME ($t_s$) 0.82 s
STEADY ERROR ($e_{ss}$) 0.00 %
SETPOINT TARGET $r(t)$: 75.0
PROPORTIONAL GAIN ($K_p$): 3.20

Drives corrective force proportional to immediate position error.

INTEGRAL GAIN ($K_i$): 1.10

Accumulates past steady-state errors to overcome gravity/friction offset.

DERIVATIVE GAIN ($K_d$): 0.65

Anticipates future error trajectory to damp resonant oscillations.
