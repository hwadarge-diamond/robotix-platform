CHAPTER 03 // HANDS-ON LAB

Hardware & Kinematics Simulators

Experience real-time interactive physics models: acoustic distance sensing, optical motor encoders, PWM servo actuation, and 2-joint forward/inverse kinematics.

LAB 01 // ACOUSTIC ECHO

Ultrasonic Rangefinder (HC-SR04)

Emits 40 kHz pulses. Measures round-trip flight duration to calculate obstacle distance: $d = \frac{v \cdot \Delta t}{2}$ ($v = 343\text{ m/s}$).

OBSTACLE DISTANCE: 120 cm
ROUND-TRIP TOF: 6997 µs
LAB 02 // ROTATION & TICKS

DC Motor & Optical Encoder

A slotted wheel interrupts a phototransistor beam to generate digital pulses, allowing the controller to calculate angular velocity and position.

TARGET VELOCITY: 1500 RPM
TICKS: 0
LAB 03 // POSITION SERVO

PWM Servo Motor Position

Hobby servos position their output shaft proportionally to pulse width: 1.0 ms (0°), 1.5 ms (90° neutral), and 2.0 ms (180° max) at 50 Hz.

SERVO ANGLE: 90°
PULSE DURATION: 1.50 ms
LAB 04 // MANIPULATOR KINEMATICS

2-Link Planar Kinematics Arm

Drag the red end-effector on the canvas to solve Inverse Kinematics (IK) via the Law of Cosines, or adjust joint sliders for Forward Kinematics.

Joint Angles Control

SHOULDER (θ1): 45°
ELBOW (θ2): 45°
FORWARD KINEMATICS FORMULAS:
x = L1 · cos(θ1) + L2 · cos(θ1 + θ2)
y = L1 · sin(θ1) + L2 · sin(θ1 + θ2)
END-EFFECTOR (X, Y): (150, 150)
